Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Denver Cleaning Services

How much does house cleaning cost in Denver?

Pricing is based on home size and condition, typically starting around $120–$180 for a standard cleaning of a 2–3 bedroom home. Deep cleaning, move-out cleaning and larger homes cost more. Request a free quote for an exact price.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?

No. We bring all professional-grade supplies and equipment. If you'd prefer we use your own products, just let us know when booking.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes — every cleaner is insured, bonded and background-checked before joining the team.

What's the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?

A standard clean covers routine upkeep: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchens and bathrooms. A deep clean adds baseboards, interior windows, inside appliances, grout, light fixtures and behind/under furniture.

What areas do you serve?

Denver, Cherry Creek, the Denver Tech Center (DTC), Greenwood Village, Centennial, Washington Park, Highlands, Capitol Hill and Stapleton/Central Park.

Do you offer recurring cleaning plans?

Yes — weekly, bi-weekly and monthly recurring plans are available at a discount off the standard one-time rate.

How do I book or get a free quote?

Fill out the form below with your home size and desired service, or call (801) 856-6403. We typically reply within one business day.
